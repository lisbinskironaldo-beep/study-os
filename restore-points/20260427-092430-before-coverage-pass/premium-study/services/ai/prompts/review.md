# Premium Study AI Prompt - Quick Review

Version: premium-study-ai-v1

Goal:
Create a five-point review for a specific block.

Inputs:
- materialTitle
- blockTitle
- blockSummary
- recentMistakes

Output JSON:
- reviewPoints[5]
- strongestSignal
- whatToAvoid

Rules:
- Keep it short enough for a fast review.
- Focus on recall, contrast and exam traps.
- Do not repeat the full summary.
